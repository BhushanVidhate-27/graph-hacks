# Google Flow — Submission Video Prompt

For **Veo 3** in Google Flow (labs.google/flow). Each scene below is a separate
generation. Flow gives you ~8s per clip at 1080p; keep each scene to one idea and
cut in the edit. Dialogue is generated only in Veo 3, so lines marked
**[dialogue]** are spoken by the actor on camera — keep them under ~12 words or
the lip-sync drifts.

---

## Global style lock — paste into every generation

```
Cinematic architectural render, warm off-white paper background (#f4f3f0), 
graphite line work, single vermilion accent (#DD6013) used sparingly, 
fine-pen technical drawing aesthetic mixed with photoreal concrete and glass, 
muted natural daylight, slow deliberate camera, shallow depth of field, 
no text overlays, no logos, no watermarks, 24fps, 16:9
```

## Edit notes

- **Music:** one sustained low drone under scenes 1–3, resolve to a single clean
  note on 6. Cut the music entirely for scene 6 — the silence is the joke.
- **Do not** add any on-screen text during scenes 1–7. The whole design is
  typographic; burned-in captions fight it and look amateur.
- **Aspect:** generate 16:9, then crop to 16:9 safe. Judge screens are often
  letterboxed in the submission portal.
- **Length:** ~60s total. Under 90s.
- **Sound design:** keep the room tone in scenes 4–6. Silence during the refusal
  reads as a failure to the viewer unless the edit makes it deliberate.

---

## Backup prompt — if Veo 3 dialogue is unavailable

Drop the `[dialogue]` lines and let the on-screen text carry it. Generate silent,
then add the narration in the edit. The visuals do not depend on the audio.
